# PART A — ANSWER KEY WITH EXPLANATIONS

**Q1. Answer: B** — A bias audit prompt systematically checks for documented bias patterns: gendered language ("ninja," "rockstar"), unnecessarily restrictive degree requirements, ageist terminology, and cultural bias. Pronoun changes alone (A) address only one narrow form of bias.

**Q2. Answer: B** — This is the most critical Module 4 distinction. AI drafts financially coherent narratives but cannot verify the accuracy of the data it is given. An AI fed wrong numbers produces a well-written narrative about wrong numbers. All financial figures must be verified by the finance professional.

**Q3. Answer: B** — Generic SWOT outputs always trace to insufficient specific context. When the prompt provides no company data (revenue, market position, named competitors), AI defaults to the most probable SWOT observations for "any company." The fix: paste specific company and market data so the AI can make evidence-based observations.

**Q4. Answer: C** — Option C specifies role, framework, length, and tone — the four elements that produce professionally structured support responses. Options A, B, and D give the AI no structural guidance.

**Q5. Answer: C** — Porter's Five Forces (1979) analyzes: Threat of new entrants, Buyer power, Supplier power, Threat of substitutes, Competitive rivalry — five forces that determine how profitable an industry is and where strategic opportunities exist.

**Q6. Answer: B** — A/B testing in content means generating two distinct versions (different hook, CTA, or angle) and measuring real audience response. AI is efficient at generating the B variant from A with one specified change.

**Q7. Answer: C** — Behavioral observations are the foundation of quality performance reviews. Without specific examples, AI generates generic language ("strong communicator") that is legally and professionally inadequate. Specific input → specific output.

**Q8. Answer: B** — Empathy-First means acknowledging the customer's experience before solving the problem. Skipping this step — even with a perfect solution — leaves customers feeling unheard, which is the primary driver of escalations.

**Q9. Answer: B** — A brand voice document must be behavioral: voice attributes (what to sound like), anti-examples (what not to sound like), few-shot examples (show the pattern concretely), forbidden words (override specific bad habits). Company facts alone do not change AI output behavior.

**Q10. Answer: B** — PESTLE: **P**olitical, **E**conomic, **S**ocial, **T**echnological, **L**egal, **E**nvironmental. A macro-environment scanning framework for strategic planning.

**Q11. Answer: B** — HR policies carry direct legal implications that vary by jurisdiction. AI-generated policies may reflect outdated or internationally generalized labor law. All HR policies require qualified legal or compliance review before adoption.

**Q12. Answer: B** — Content repurposing converts one research investment into multiple platform-specific outputs: blog → LinkedIn → email → Instagram → Twitter thread. AI multiplies content output without multiplying research effort.

**Q13. Answer: B** — AI supports financial modeling through documentation, explanation, and code generation — not the calculations themselves. Attempting to use AI for financial arithmetic without independent verification is high-risk.

**Q14. Answer: A** — AIDA: **A**wareness (hook attention), **I**nterest (build engagement), **D**esire (create want through benefits), **A**ction (specific CTA). The classic direct-response progression from attention to conversion.

**Q15. Answer: C** — The RAG principle applied to competitor analysis: paste primary public sources (annual report, press releases, job postings) directly into the prompt. AI's training knowledge (A) about specific companies is often outdated or incomplete.

**Q16. Answer: B** — Escalation triggers are predefined conditions that override AI autonomy regardless of capability: customer requests human, legal threat mentioned, security concern raised, same issue third occurrence, customer distress. These require human judgment.

**Q17. Answer: B** — AI-generated SQL must be reviewed by someone with SQL expertise before execution. Data modification queries (UPDATE, DELETE) can cause irreversible damage; complex JOINs may silently produce incorrect results.

**Q18. Answer: C** — A professional business case requires a quantified financial argument: costs, benefits, NPV/ROI, and stated assumptions. Without quantification, it is an opinion document. All numbers must be independently verified.

**Q19. Answer: B** — AI's role in 360-degree feedback is synthesis assistant: anonymized raw feedback → themed narrative → HR review before delivery. AI does not collect feedback, score colleagues, or identify respondents.

**Q20. Answer: B** — A complete SEO blog prompt specifies: keyword, audience, search intent (informational/commercial), word count, required H2 headers, and natural placement guidance. Keyword stuffing (A) is a penalized practice.

**Q21. Answer: B** — Brand voice consistency requires systematic implementation: every content prompt includes the brand voice document as context input, regardless of who built the prompt or what format is being generated.

**Q22. Answer: B** — RAG grounding is mandatory for product-specific FAQ. AI improvising answers from general knowledge will hallucinate features, procedures, or policies that don't exist — creating support problems, not preventing them.

**Q23. Answer: B** — A minimum viable market entry analysis: (1) Market opportunity — is it worth entering?, (2) Competitive landscape — who is there and how strong?, (3) Barriers to entry — what makes entry difficult? All three are decision-critical.

**Q24. Answer: B** — Cornerstone content is the high-quality long-form piece (blog post, white paper, guide) that serves as the source material for all repurposed formats. The research investment happens once; AI handles the transformation into multiple formats.

**Q25. Answer: B** — Negotiation preparation using AI is most valuable for mapping the other party's BATNA, priorities, and likely concessions — strategic intelligence that improves your position. A word-for-word script (A) would be unnatural and inflexible in a live negotiation.

**B1.** Why is a bias audit prompt essential after generating a job description? Name three specific bias types it should check for. *(2 marks)*

**Answer:** Job descriptions are gatekeeping documents — biased language silently reduces the diversity of applicants before any human reviews a single CV. Three specific bias types to audit: (1) **Gendered language** — words like "rockstar," "ninja," "aggressive," or "dominant" code masculine and deter female applicants; words like "nurturing" or "collaborative" may deter male applicants in certain contexts. (2) **Over-qualification requirements** — requiring a degree for a role where experience would suffice, or requiring 10 years of experience for mid-level roles, systematically excludes qualified candidates from underrepresented backgrounds. (3) **Ageist language** — "digital native," "young and dynamic," or "recent graduate preferred" signal age preference, which is illegal in many jurisdictions.

*Marking: 1 mark for why bias auditing matters. 1 mark for three specific, correctly identified bias types.*

**B3.** Explain why "Let me know if you need anything else" is a weak customer support closing, and write a stronger AI prompt instruction to replace it. *(2 marks)*

**Answer:** "Let me know if you need anything else" is weak because: it places the burden of next action entirely on the customer, it does not confirm that the current issue is resolved, it signals the agent is done caring rather than committed to resolution, and it is so generic that it adds no value. A stronger closing follows the "Follow-up" step of AEERF: confirm the resolution, state what happens next, and provide a specific re-contact path.

**Better instruction:**
```
Close every support response with:
1. A resolution confirmation ("This should now be fully resolved")
   OR a clear next step with timeline ("You'll receive X within Y hours")
2. A specific re-contact instruction: "If this isn't resolved by [timeframe],
   reply to this email directly and I'll escalate personally"
Never use: "Let me know if you need anything else" — it is vague and passive.
```

*Marking: 1 mark for correctly diagnosing why the phrase fails. 1 mark for a prompt instruction that produces a genuinely stronger closing.*

**B5.** What is the difference between TAM, SAM, and SOM? Why does a business case using only TAM mislead decision-makers? *(2 marks)*

**Answer:** **TAM** (Total Addressable Market) is the total global revenue opportunity if you captured 100% of the market — a theoretical ceiling. **SAM** (Serviceable Addressable Market) is the segment you can realistically reach with your current product model, pricing, and geography. **SOM** (Serviceable Obtainable Market) is what you can realistically win given your current resources, team, and competitive position — typically 1–5% of SAM in early stages. Using only TAM misleads decision-makers because: a ₹500Cr TAM implies massive opportunity, but if the SAM is ₹50Cr (only India, only enterprise) and the SOM is ₹5Cr (realistic capture in 3 years), the investment thesis changes completely. Presenting TAM without SAM and SOM is a common pitch error that experienced investors immediately question.

*Marking: 1 mark for accurate definitions of all three with the relationship between them. 1 mark for the specific misleading consequence of TAM-only presentation.*

**B7.** What are three tasks in HR where AI assistance is INAPPROPRIATE without significant human oversight? *(2 marks)*

**Answer:** (1) **Final hiring decisions** — AI screening tools have documented demographic bias and shortlist errors; legal accountability for hiring discrimination rests with the employer, not the AI. Humans must make the final call with full judgment. (2) **Disciplinary or termination decisions** — these require legal process compliance, proportionality assessment, and procedural documentation specific to jurisdiction and company policy. An AI-generated termination notice without legal review is a significant liability. (3) **Disability accommodation assessments** — determining reasonable accommodation requires medical information, ADA/local law knowledge, and individualized human judgment. AI cannot assess what accommodations are appropriate for a specific employee's condition without crossing privacy and legal boundaries.

**B8.** How would you prompt AI to generate a BATNA analysis before a supplier pricing negotiation? *(2 marks)*

**Answer:**
```
You are a negotiation strategist preparing me for a supplier pricing renewal.

when to walk away.
```

**B9.** What makes an AI-generated marketing campaign brief useful vs. generic? Give three specific elements that must be in the prompt to produce a useful brief. *(2 marks)*

**Answer:** An AI-generated campaign brief is generic when it contains observations that apply to any brand ("target millennials," "use social media," "create engaging content"). (3) **Creative guardrails from the brand voice document** — paste the brand voice card and add: "Every creative asset must be reviewable against this voice standard before production."

**C4.** *(6 marks)* Design a complete AI-assisted customer support system: AI persona, 3 response templates, quality rubric, and escalation policy. Explain why each design decision was made.

**Answer:**

**AI Persona (System Prompt):**
```
You are Aria, AI support assistant for CloudDesk (project management SaaS).

HANDLE INDEPENDENTLY:
- Feature questions and how-to guidance
- Account access and login (standard process)
- Billing questions (explain plans; cannot process refunds)
- Standard integration troubleshooting

ESCALATE IMMEDIATELY (do not attempt to resolve):
- Refund requests over ₹5,000 → billing specialist
- Security/data breach → security team (URGENT)
- Customer waiting > 48 hours unresolved → senior agent
- Legal/compliance mention → legal team
- Customer explicitly requests human

RESPONSE STRUCTURE: Acknowledge → Empathize → Explain → Resolve → Follow-up
TONE: Warm, direct, human. Use first name when known. Under 150 words (chat).
Never say "I understand your frustration" — show understanding specifically.
Never start with "Certainly!" or "Of course!"
```

**3 Response Templates:**

*Feature Question:*
```
Hi {NAME}, great question about {FEATURE}.
{3-step how-to}
{Optional: "Video walkthrough here: [link]"}
Does this get you where you need to be? Happy to walk through it together.
```

*Billing Inquiry:*
```
Hi {NAME}, thanks for reaching out about {BILLING TOPIC}.
{Specific explanation of what happened and why}
{Specific next action and timeline}
If this isn't what you expected, reply here directly and I'll sort it personally.
```

*Technical Issue:*
```
Hi {NAME}, I can see why {SPECIFIC ISSUE} would be frustrating — let's fix this.
Try these steps:
1. {STEP}   2. {STEP}   3. {STEP}
If unresolved, send me {SPECIFIC DIAGNOSTIC INFO} and I'll escalate with everything
the technical team needs. I'll follow up by {TIMEFRAME}.
```

**Quality Rubric:**

| Criterion | 1 (Poor) | 3 (Adequate) | 5 (Excellent) |
|-----------|---------|-------------|--------------|
| Empathy | None | Generic | Specific to this customer |
| Accuracy | Incorrect | Mostly correct | Fully accurate vs. documentation |
| Resolution | None provided | Partial | Clear resolution with timeline |
| AEERF adherence | 2+ steps missing | 3/5 present | All 5 present |
| Brevity | Padded or too short | Acceptable | Every sentence does work |

Target: ≥4.0 average. Any ≤2 triggers coaching. System average <3.5 triggers prompt revision.

**Escalation Policy:**

| Trigger | Action | SLA |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Human requested | Transfer immediately | 5 min |
| Security/fraud concern | Security team — urgent flag | 15 min |
| Refund > ₹5,000 | Billing specialist | 2 hours |
| Same issue, 3rd report | Senior agent + full context | 1 hour |
| Legal mention | Legal team + manager flag | 30 min |

**Design decisions:**
- Limitation list is more detailed than capability list — undefined boundaries cause AI to improvise, creating false promises and legal risk.
- Templates use variable placeholders for consistency — the AI fills in specifics rather than generating from scratch each time.
- Escalation SLAs are specific minutes/hours — "quickly" is not an SLA.
- Quality rubric mirrors the AEERF framework — evaluation criteria match the response structure standard.

**C5.** *(6 marks)* Calculate the ROI of automating invoice processing commentary with AI. Current state: 3 analysts × 8 hours/week × ₹800/hour fully loaded. Design the calculation framework and the prompt that would produce automation-ready output.

**Answer:**

**ROI Calculation Framework:**

**Step 1 — Current State Cost:**
- Weekly hours: 3 analysts × 8 hrs = 24 hours/week
- Weekly cost: 24 × ₹800 = ₹19,200/week
- Annual cost: ₹19,200 × 50 weeks = **₹9,60,000/year**

**Step 2 — AI-Enabled State (conservative 65% time reduction):**
- Remaining human time: 24 hrs × 35% = 8.4 hours/week (review + corrections)
- Remaining human cost: 8.4 × ₹800 × 50 = ₹3,36,000/year
- AI tool cost (API): ~₹1,500/week × 50 = ₹75,000/year
- Implementation (one-time): Prompt development, testing, training = ₹50,000

**Step 3 — ROI Calculation:**
- Annual saving: ₹9,60,000 − ₹3,36,000 − ₹75,000 = **₹5,49,000/year**
- First-year net (after implementation): ₹5,49,000 − ₹50,000 = **₹4,99,000**
- ROI Year 1: ₹4,99,000 / ₹1,25,000 (total new cost) = **399%**
- Payback period: Implementation cost recovered in **~5 weeks**

**Step 4 — Risk Adjustment:**
- Quality risk: 5% of AI drafts require significant rework → add 1.2 hrs/week buffer
- Compliance risk: Invoice commentary reviewed by finance lead before submission
- Net adjusted saving: ~₹4,80,000/year (conservative)
