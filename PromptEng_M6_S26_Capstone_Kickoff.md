## 26.1 What is the Capstone Project?

The capstone project is your opportunity to synthesize everything learned across 25 sessions into a coherent, real-world AI productivity solution. It is the evidence that you have moved from understanding concepts to applying them professionally.

### What a Strong Capstone Looks Like

```
STRONG CAPSTONE CHARACTERISTICS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
✓ Solves a REAL problem from your actual professional context
✓ Demonstrates MULTIPLE techniques (not just one prompt)
✓ Includes a PROMPT LIBRARY with at least 8 documented entries
✓ Shows ITERATION — version history, what changed, why
✓ Applies RESPONSIBLE AI principles (bias audit, privacy considerations)
✓ Produces MEASURABLE IMPACT — time saved, quality improved, task enabled
✓ Is PRESENTABLE — a working system someone else could use

WEAK CAPSTONE CHARACTERISTICS:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
✗ A collection of one-off prompts with no coherent use case
✗ Demonstrates only the simplest techniques (only zero-shot)
✗ No iteration documented — first draft submitted as final
✗ Generic — could apply to "any company" with no real context
✗ No measurement of impact
✗ Only the student can use it — no documentation for reuse
```

## 26.2 Capstone Project Options

Choose one of the following tracks based on your professional context:

---

### Track A: AI Productivity System for Your Role

**What it is:** A complete, documented AI productivity system for your specific professional role — covering your most time-intensive repeating tasks.

**Minimum requirements:**
- Workday audit identifying top 8+ AI opportunities
- 10+ prompt library entries (full documentation standard)
- 3+ workflow designs (morning routine, core task type, end-of-day)
- Automation design for at least 1 recurring task
- Measurable impact: before/after time comparison for 5 tasks
- Responsible AI compliance checklist for your context

**Best for:** Professionals focused on personal productivity transformation

---

### Track B: AI Solution for a Business Function

**What it is:** A purpose-built AI solution addressing a specific business function (marketing, HR, finance, customer support, strategy).

**Minimum requirements:**
- Problem statement and business case
- 10+ prompt library entries specific to the function
- At least 1 multi-step pipeline (minimum 4 steps)
- Sample outputs with quality evaluation (rubric scoring)
- Automation design for the highest-volume task
- Responsible AI assessment (bias audit + privacy review)
- ROI calculation

**Best for:** Professionals building team or organizational AI capabilities

---

### Track C: AI-Powered Content or Knowledge System

**What it is:** A comprehensive content production or knowledge management system powered by AI prompting.

**Minimum requirements:**
- Content strategy with 4-week calendar
- Complete content production pipeline (brief to published)
- 10+ prompt library entries covering all content formats needed
- Brand voice documentation and few-shot examples
- Repurposing pipeline (1 cornerstone → 5+ formats)
- Quality rubric for content evaluation
- Responsible AI: bias audit on representative outputs

**Best for:** Content creators, marketers, communications professionals

---

### Track D: AI Learning and Assessment System

**What it is:** An AI-assisted learning content and assessment system for a topic you are an expert in.

**Minimum requirements:**
- Learning objectives and curriculum design (5+ modules)
- AI-assisted content generation pipeline for 3 lesson types
- Assessment bank: 20+ questions (MCQ, scenario, practical)
- Student feedback response templates
- Personalization prompts (adapting content for different learner levels)
- Quality standards for educational AI content
- Responsible AI: accessibility and bias considerations

**Best for:** Trainers, educators, L&D professionals, subject matter experts

## 26.5 Using AI to Plan Your Capstone

Apply your prompt engineering skills to help design the capstone itself.

### Capstone Problem Ideation Prompt

```
You are a curriculum designer and professional coach helping a student
select and define a meaningful capstone project for their AI certification.

Student profile:
- Role: [YOUR ROLE]
- Industry: [YOUR INDUSTRY]
- Organization: [DESCRIBE YOUR ORGANIZATION]
- Key responsibilities: [LIST 5]
- Biggest time consumers at work: [LIST 3–5]
- Most frustrating repeating tasks: [LIST 2–3]

Help me define a strong capstone project:

1. PROBLEM IDENTIFICATION: Based on my profile, what are the 3 most
   compelling problems I could solve with AI? Rate each for:
   - Impact potential (High/Medium/Low)
   - Feasibility in 5 sessions (High/Medium/Low)
   - Learning demonstration value (High/Medium/Low)

2. RECOMMENDED TRACK: Which of the 4 capstone tracks (A/B/C/D) best fits
   my role and what will have most impact?

3. PROBLEM STATEMENT: Draft a Problem Definition for the top recommendation
   using the Problem Definition Document structure.

4. SUCCESS METRICS: Suggest 3 specific, measurable success metrics for
   this project that I can establish a baseline for immediately.

5. QUICK WINS: What can I implement in the FIRST session (Session 26)
   to get immediate value even before the full project is complete?
```

### Capstone Scope Validation Prompt

```
Review my capstone project scope and tell me if it's too big, too small,
or appropriately scoped for 5 sessions of work (approximately 25–30 hours total).

My Problem Definition Document: [PASTE YOUR DRAFT PDD]

Evaluate:
1. SCOPE SIZE: Is this achievable in 5 sessions? Too ambitious? Too narrow?
   If too big: what should I cut to maintain quality?
   If too small: what additional dimension would strengthen the project?

2. TECHNIQUE COVERAGE: Does this project allow me to demonstrate the
   full range of techniques from the course? Which techniques are underrepresented?

3. MEASURABILITY: Are my success metrics truly measurable? Can I realistically
   gather before/after data?

4. DIFFERENTIATION: What would make this project genuinely impressive vs.
   adequate? What single addition would most strengthen it?

5. RISK ASSESSMENT: What is the biggest risk that could derail this project?
   Provide a specific mitigation strategy.
```

## 26.6 Capstone Assessment Rubric — Full Criteria

Understanding how your project will be assessed allows you to build toward the highest quality from day one.

```
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CAPSTONE ASSESSMENT RUBRIC
Total: 100 points
Pass mark: 70 points
Distinction: 85+ points
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SECTION 1: PROBLEM DEFINITION & PLANNING (15 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ Problem statement is specific, real, and professionally meaningful (5)
□ Scope is appropriate — not too broad, not too narrow (5)
□ Success metrics are measurable with realistic baseline and target (5)

SECTION 2: PROMPT LIBRARY QUALITY (25 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ Minimum 10 complete entries (full documentation standard) (10)
□ Demonstrates variety of techniques across entries (5)
□ All entries have tested examples with quality ratings (5)
□ Iteration history shows real improvement journey (5)

SECTION 3: TECHNICAL DEPTH (20 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ At least one multi-step pipeline with quality gates (8)
□ Advanced techniques applied correctly (CoT, Persona, RAG, etc.) (7)
□ Automation design is realistic and implementable (5)

SECTION 4: QUALITY AND IMPACT (20 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ Outputs demonstrate clear quality improvement over baseline (10)
□ Measurable impact documented (time saved, quality improvement) (5)
□ Quality evaluation rubric applied to representative outputs (5)

SECTION 5: RESPONSIBLE AI (10 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ Bias audit conducted and findings documented (5)
□ Privacy considerations addressed appropriately (3)
□ Professional accountability standards applied (2)

SECTION 6: PRESENTATION & COMMUNICATION (10 points)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ 10-minute presentation is structured and compelling (5)
□ Message headlines (not topic labels) used throughout (2)
□ Q&A handled professionally with specific, honest answers (3)

BONUS (up to 5 additional points):
□ Exceptionally novel or creative application of AI techniques (+3)
□ Team implementation documented (built for others to use) (+2)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```
